1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to website owners.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for the Site to function properly (page navigation, security, access to certain areas).
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our Site by collecting and reporting information anonymously.
  • Functionality Cookies: Allow the Site to remember choices you make (such as language preference or light/dark mode).
  • Marketing Cookies: Used to track visitors across websites to display ads that are relevant and engaging.
