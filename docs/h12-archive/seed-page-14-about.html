About SINO FRESH

A private label pet supplement manufacturer built on R&D, quality, and reliable delivery.

Who We Are

Shandong SINO FRESH Pet Food Co., Ltd. is a private label pet supplement manufacturer based in Linyi, Shandong, China. We help brand owners, distributors, and retailers launch and scale their own pet supplement lines under their own brands.

Our 15,000 m² facility includes a 6,000 m² warehouse, a 1,000 m² R&D laboratory, and a 500 m² showroom. Production runs in ISO 8 cleanrooms across eight dosage-form lines: soft chews, tablets, powders, pastes, drops, liquids, fish oil, and dental chews.

With more than 100 production and testing machines, we control every step in-house — from formula development and raw material inspection to blending, filling, and finished-goods testing. Every batch ships with a Certificate of Analysis.

We are 100% OEM/ODM. We never sell our own retail brand, so your formula, your branding, and your market always stay yours.

Our Journey

2016

Production began

Founding team started pet supplement production under an earlier company entity, building hands-on experience in formulation, production, and quality control.

2017

OEM projects expanded

Team delivered private-label projects for domestic brands and early export clients, gaining experience in packaging, labeling, and international documentation.

2025

SINO FRESH founded

Shandong SINO FRESH Pet Food Co., Ltd. established in Linyi, Shandong, with a single focus: 100% OEM/ODM pet supplement manufacturing.

2025

Factory commissioned

15,000 m² facility comes online with ISO 8 cleanrooms and eight dosage-form production lines: soft chews, tablets, powders, pastes, drops, liquids, fish oil, and dental chews.

2025

R&D center established

1,000 m² R&D laboratory and 200+ production and testing equipment put into operation, supporting formula development, palatability testing, and stability studies.

2026

Certifications completed

FDA, cGMP, ISO 9001, FSSC 22000, HACCP, and BRC certifications obtained, opening US and EU markets.

2026

Exporting worldwide

Private label products ship to brand owners and distributors in 30+ countries across North America, Europe, and Asia.

2026

Ongoing growth

Continuous expansion of production capacity, dosage-form capabilities, and global client base.
