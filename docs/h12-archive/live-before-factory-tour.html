Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a factory tour take?

On-site tours usually take 2–3 hours. Video tours take about 45–60 minutes.

Yes. We encourage you to bring your R&D, QC, or compliance team.

Do you support third-party audits?

Yes. We support third-party audits and can provide documentation in advance.

Is the video tour live?

Yes. We use WhatsApp, Zoom, or WeChat for live video tours.

Can I see the QC laboratory?

Yes. The QC laboratory is part of every tour.
