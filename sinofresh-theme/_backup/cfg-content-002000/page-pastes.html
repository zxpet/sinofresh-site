Frequently Asked Questions

What is the MOQ for pastes?

Standard formulas start from 500 units. New brands can start with small trial orders.

How long does sampling take?

3–7 working days for standard formulas.

Can you customize tube weight and volume?

Yes. We support custom tube weights and volumes.

Do you offer hairball and nutritional pastes on the same line?

Yes. Both can be produced on the same line with proper changeover procedures.

Do you provide COA for each batch?

Yes. Every batch is tested in our QC laboratory and ships with a COA.

Do you support private label?

Yes. We are 100% OEM/ODM. No brand conflict.
