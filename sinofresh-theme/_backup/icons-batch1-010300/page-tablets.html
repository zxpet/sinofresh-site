Frequently Asked Questions

What is the MOQ for tablets?

Standard formulas start from 1,000 units. New brands can start with small trial orders.

Can you customize tablet weight and count per bottle?

Yes. We support custom weight per tablet and count per container.

Do you provide COA for each batch?

Yes. Every batch is tested in our QC laboratory and ships with a COA.

Do you support private label?

Yes. We are 100% OEM/ODM. No brand conflict.

What packaging options do you offer?
