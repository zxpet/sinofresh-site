About SINO FRESH

A private label pet supplement manufacturer built on R&D, quality, and reliable delivery.

Who We Are

SINO FRESH Pet Food Co., Ltd. is a private label pet supplement manufacturer based in Linyi, Shandong, China. We help brand owners, distributors, and retailers launch and scale their own pet supplement lines under their own brands.

Our 15,000 m² facility includes a 6,000 m² warehouse, a 1,000 m² R&D laboratory, and a 300 m² showroom. Production runs in 100,000-class clean workshops across five dosage-form lines: soft chews, tablets, powders, pastes, and liquids.

With more than 100 production and testing machines, we control every step in-house — from formula development and raw material inspection to blending, filling, and finished-goods testing. Every batch ships with a Certificate of Analysis.

We are 100% OEM/ODM. We never sell our own retail brand, so your formula, your branding, and your market always stay yours.
