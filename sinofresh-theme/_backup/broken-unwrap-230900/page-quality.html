Frequently Asked Questions

Is your facility FDA registered?

Yes. Our facility is registered with the U.S. Food and Drug Administration for pet supplement manufacturing.

What does cGMP mean for pet supplements?

cGMP ensures products are consistently produced and controlled according to quality standards.

Do you provide a COA for every batch?

Yes. Every batch is tested in our QC laboratory and ships with a Certificate of Analysis.

What testing capabilities do you have in-house?

HPLC, GC, and AAS for active content, fatty acid profiles, and heavy metal testing.

Do you retain samples?

Yes. Retention samples are kept for every batch under controlled conditions.

Can I request specific testing items on my COA?

Yes. If your target market requires additional testing, we can add them.
