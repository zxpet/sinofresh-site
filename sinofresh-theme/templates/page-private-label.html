How Private Label Works

Private label is the fastest route to market when development time is the constraint.

You choose from our ready-to-market recipes instead of commissioning a new one, and the product ships under your brand. Because the recipe already exists, the timeline belongs to packaging and labelling rather than to development — which is what makes it work for a first order, a test market or a seasonal line.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I put my own brand on a standard formula?

Yes. That is exactly what private label means: the recipe is ours, the brand on the finished product is yours.

What is the minimum order for a new brand?

Low MOQ for new brands. MOQ starts at 500–1,000 units depending on dosage form, and small trial orders are welcome.

How fast can I launch?

Sampling takes 3–7 working days, and bulk lead time is 7–15 working days after packaging is ready. No development time is needed because the recipe already exists.
