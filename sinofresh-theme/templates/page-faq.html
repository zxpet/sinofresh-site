Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about MOQ, sampling, lead times, certifications, customization, and shipping. Anything else — our team replies within one working day.

Ordering & MOQ

What is your MOQ?

From 500 units depending on dosage form. New brands can start with small trial orders.

How long does sampling take?

3–7 working days for standard formulas. Complex formulas may take longer.

What is the sampling fee?

$200, deductible from bulk order.

How long is bulk lead time?

7–15 working days after packaging ready.

Can I start with a small trial order?

Yes. We support small trial orders for new brands and new products.

Certifications & Compliance

What certifications do you hold?

FDA registration, cGMP, ISO 9001, FSSC 22000, HACCP, BRC.

Is your facility FDA registered?

Yes. Our facility is registered with the U.S. Food and Drug Administration for pet supplement manufacturing.

Can you handle regulatory documentation for my market?

Yes. We support FDA, EU, and other target market requirements.

Do you provide COA for every batch?

Yes. Every batch is tested in our QC laboratory and ships with a Certificate of Analysis.

What testing capabilities do you have in-house?

HPLC, GC, and AAS for active content, fatty acid profiles, and heavy metal testing.

Do you retain samples?

Yes. Retention samples are kept for every batch under controlled conditions.
