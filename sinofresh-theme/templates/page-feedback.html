Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to get a response?

Every submission is reviewed by our team within 2 business days. Product and partnership inquiries receive a follow-up within 24 hours on business days.

Can I submit feedback anonymously?

Yes. The email field is optional — leave it blank and your message will be treated as anonymous. Add an email only if you would like a personal reply.

What types of feedback do you accept?

General feedback, website issues, product inquiries, and partnership proposals. For quotes and samples, please use the contact form so our sales team can route your request faster.
