How OEM Works

OEM is the model for brands that already know exactly what they want to make.

You supply the formula and the specification sheet; we source to spec, blend, form, test and deliver — without changing a number in the recipe. It is the fastest route to production, because development is already behind you. Our job is execution at scale, with the batch records, certificates and export paperwork that importers and regulators ask for.

Frequently Asked Questions

What do you need from me for a quotation?

Your formula, the specification sheet, your packaging requirements and your target market. We come back with an itemized quotation and a sampling plan.

What is the minimum order?

MOQ starts at 500–1,000 units depending on dosage form. Small trial orders are welcome for new brands.

How long does sampling and production take?

Sampling takes 3–7 working days and is deductible from bulk. Bulk lead time is 7–15 working days after packaging is ready.
