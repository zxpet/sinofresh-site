How Contract Manufacturing Works

Contract manufacturing is the model for brands that have everything written down.

You bring the formula, the specification, the packaging and the regulatory route for each market. Nothing about the product is ours to decide — our role is the production line. That makes this the model for scaled, repeat orders: the same specification batch after batch, with the batch records and certificates your distributor or importer needs.
